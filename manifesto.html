Question bank

Clarity: What exactly do you mean by…?
Problems: What could be a problem with this policy?
Fairness: Who benefits from this?
Consequences: What would happen if…?
Disagreement: Why should this apply only to that group?
Cooperation: Would you consider working with us on…?

Campaign rules

Speak English. Everyone participates. Use keywords, not full scripts. Stay in character and use your party's register. Challenge ideas, not people. Listen carefully. Be ready for unexpected questions.
