Language help · Build an agreement
  • We have agreed to work with…
  • Our shared priority is…
  • Our party can contribute…
  • We are willing to change… if…
  • Our joint policy would…
  • In practice, this would work by…
  • We have reached an agreement on…
Teacher quick start
  1. Bring the already chosen partner parties together. Select one party to keep the pact’s shared notes, then select its partners.
  2. Identify a shared priority using concrete policies. Each party explains what it brings to the agreement.
  3. Build one joint policy together. Explain who benefits, how it would work and what each party agrees to change or compromise on.
  4. Review the agreement together and present it briefly. Every party should contribute to the explanation.

The aim is to turn existing alliances into a concrete pact and a new shared policy. Keep notes brief and discuss decisions aloud. Each text field allows 999 characters.
