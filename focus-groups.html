Campaign Day 2 · Listen. Adapt. Convince.

Voter Focus Groups.

Listen → Respond → Handle an objection → Give a verdict.
One screen for the class. Students speak; the teacher controls the buttons. Open “Teacher tools” below for the role setup.

    Main concerns

    Voters: explain these concerns in your own words.
    Campaign team: listen, then explain how one or two of your policies could help.

    Voter objection

    Answer the objection. Then ask:

    A different speaker answers.

    Voters: convinced, partly convinced or not convinced? Give one reason aloud.

    After the party’s first answer, show the objection. Then you can move to the next voter.

    What did we learn?

    What should your party explain better after listening to voters?

    Discuss together, then share a brief oral response with the class.

      Teacher tools · Optional

      Before you begin, assign one group to play the voter and one party to respond. The other students listen for whether the answer addresses the concern. You can swap roles between encounters. Reveal the objection after the party’s first response; move on after the oral verdict. No student logins, forms or saved verdicts are required.
