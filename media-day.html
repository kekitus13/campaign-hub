Press conference.

The media controls the questions.

Teacher quick start · Run this session
  1. Select the presenting party. Its assigned journalist groups ask the questions; other groups listen.
  2. Choose two likely pressure policies. A short media message is optional. Use the 30-second opening, then open the floor.
  3. Reveal one media card. A journalist creates a question and the party answers aloud. Use Follow-Up to probe the answer, Different Speaker to share turns, or Pressure Question to challenge it (maximum two).
  4. Finish with the 30-second final message and move to the next party. Timers start only when you press Start timer; reaching zero does not move the activity on.
  5. After the conferences, reveal Breaking News. Switch the party selector to record each oral decision, then collect brief debrief keywords for each group.

Students do not need to operate the website during speaking. Saved policies appear only on the browser where they were entered or imported through Manifesto Launch. Progress saves here, but a reloaded timer starts again. Close the private notebook before projecting.

Language help · Answer, clarify, bridge

Answer first. Then return to your message. Bridge does not mean avoiding the question.

Direct answer

Yes / No, because…
Our position is that…
What we are proposing is…

Acknowledge

That’s a fair question.
I understand that concern.
That’s something we have considered.

Clarify

If I understand your question correctly…
Do you mean…?
I think we need to distinguish between…

Develop

The main reason is…
For example,…
In practice,…

Connect back

The important point is…
This connects to our main priority because…
What we want voters to remember is…
